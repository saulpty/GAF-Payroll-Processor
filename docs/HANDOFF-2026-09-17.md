## The two findings that shaped it

1. **The right feed is Teramind's Time Records grid** (`POST /tt/r/time-records/grid`) — the screen Tim
   has always exported from. Saul pointed at it. It is **live for today**, filters to our agents,
   returns exact instants. The login-session feed the VP's app uses is wrong for payroll (33% match).
2. **It reproduces payroll.** All 12 periods, 5,663 employee-days: 96% match to the minute overall,
   **99.6% on the last five periods**, entry time exact on 98.6% of days. The rest is explained (hand
   edits, last-day runs, outage days, time-off days). Full write-up:
   `docs/findings/2026-09-17-teramind-api-vs-past-periods.md`.
