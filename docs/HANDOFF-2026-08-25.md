## 1. What changed today — weekend schedules

Tim reported that some employees are on a weekend schedule and asked what rules
the app has for them. The answer turned out to be: the rule existed, and nobody
was using it.

### The rule

`schedules.work_days` — a CSV of day abbreviations per schedule, default
`'Mon,Tue,Wed,Thu,Fri'`, added by migration `1781402000`. The classification
engine honours it at `classificationEngine.ts:462`: a non-scheduled day is
skipped silently, an off-day punch with no form is dropped, and an off-day with
a Monday form becomes YELLOW for operator review. Holidays still apply, because
the holiday check runs after the work-day check.

### What was wrong

Three weekend schedules were already defined and correct:

| id | Name | `work_days` |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | Weekend Schedule Mon-Tue OFF | `Wed,Thu,Fri,Sat,Sun` |
| 11 | Weekend Schedule Tue-Wed OFF | `Mon,Thu,Fri,Sat,Sun` |
| 12 | Weekend Schedule Thu-Fri OFF | `Mon,Tue,Wed,Sat,Sun` |

**No employee was assigned to any of them.** All 50 sat on schedules 1/2/3. The
Schedules tab cannot assign anyone — it only defines schedules, while its
"pick an employee" datalist merely names one. Assignment lives in Admin →
Employees → Roster. That trap is now `BACKLOG.md` #10.

Consequence: the engine built Mon–Fri entries for four people who do not work
Mon–Fri, found no punches, and marked them `Ausencia Injustificada` / RED, while
discarding their real Saturday and Sunday work.

### Fixed today

**Assignment** (Saul, in Admin → Employees → Roster) — verified live:

| Employee | id | schedule |
|---|---|---|
| Cemiriamiz Iglesias | 46 | 11 |
| Euclides Gonzalez | 49 | 10 |
| Michael Antonio Jones Roye | 50 | 10 |
| Edwin Broce | 51 | 12 |

**Migration `1781900100`** — `v_attendance_daily` now filters on each employee's
own `work_days` instead of `WHERE EXTRACT(isodow FROM work_date) < 6`, so
weekend workers are no longer invisible on the Attendance dashboard. The `base`
CTE `LEFT JOIN`s `schedules` and exposes
`COALESCE(NULLIF(TRIM(s.work_days), ''), 'Mon,Tue,Wed,Thu,Fri')`; the final
`WHERE` does an ISO-DOW membership test against a literal `Mon..Sun` array.
`TO_CHAR(work_date,'Dy')` was deliberately avoided — it is `lc_time`-dependent.
The nine output columns are unchanged.

Verified: the diff between the old and new view bodies is exactly three hunks,
every other line byte-identical to `1781401300`; the export diff was one added
migration plus the `applied.txt` line UIB writes itself; 96/96 tests pass; and
the Attendance page was loaded and compared figure-by-figure against a
pre-change screenshot.

**Effect, measured on the Attendance page:**

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Days tracked | 788 | 784 |
| On time | 488 | 485 |
| Excused | 78 | 59 |
| Permission | 59 | 48 |

34 employee-days dropped out — weekday rows that are now correctly off-days. The
four employees went from looking like chronic absentees to:

| Employee | Days | On time | % |
|---|---|---|---|
| Euclides Gonzalez | 6 | 6 | 100% |
| Michael A. Jones Roye | 6 | 5 | 83% |
| Edwin Broce | 13 | 12 | 92% |

**`tests/weekendSchedule.test.ts`** — 9 tests, the first coverage `work_days`
has ever had. W7 is the regression test for the bug above; it includes a control
case on a Mon–Fri schedule so it cannot pass vacuously. Suite is now 96.

## 3. Still open

### Blocked on a Teramind export — needs Saul

The four employees' **Saturday and Sunday work is still not in
`payroll_entries`.** The engine skipped those days at process time, and the view
can only show rows that exist. Their weekend days only appear after their
periods are reprocessed — which requires uploading the original Teramind export
for each affected period on the Process page. Periods affected: everything since
2026-07-21.

Until that happens the four show only their weekday portion, which is why
Euclides reads 6 days rather than ~13.

### Needs an owner ruling — do not build first

Both change numbers for the 44 employees who are **not** on a weekend schedule.

- **`loadHrkSummary.ts:30`** — `base_hours` counts DOW 1–6 × 8h, i.e. Mon–**Sat**.
  This looks deliberate (Panama's 48-hour six-day week), not a bug. Switching it
  to `work_days` would drop Saturday for every Mon–Fri employee. Question: is the
  six-day base the intended convention, and what is the correct base for a
  five-day weekend shift?
- **`loadHrkSummary.ts:44-54`** — PTO appends Sat+Sun after a Friday, hardcoding
  `EXTRACT(DOW) = 5`. For a Sat–Wed worker the equivalent day is Wednesday. Depends
  on the answer above.
- **`ptoAccrual.defaultTotalDays`** — PTO is counted in calendar days, matching
  the Excel sheet. For a Sat–Wed worker a Wed→Mon leave charges 5 days where only
  3 are working days. Changing this re-bases every existing balance, so it is a
  migration question.

### Needs explicit permission — a protected file

- **`ProcessPayroll.tsx:343-357`** — the data-quality coverage check builds one
  global `expectedWorkdays` list that skips Sat/Sun, so weekend employees will
  always trigger a false "covers only 0/N workdays" warning. Warning-only, no
  money. But `ProcessPayroll.tsx` is on the never-edit list in `CLAUDE.md`, so it
  needs Saul to ask for it directly. The fix reuses `isScheduledWorkDay` from
  `classificationEngine.ts:32`, which would need exporting — also a protected
  file.
