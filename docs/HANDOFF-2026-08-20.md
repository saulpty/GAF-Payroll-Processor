## 1. What is live in the app

### PTO Tracker — `/pto`

One table, one row per active employee. No tabs.

Columns: Employee · Title · Start · Accrued · Taken · Available · Paid PTO ·
FH left · WFH · Birthday · Pending. Header sticks while rows scroll. Every
column header sorts three ways (A→Z, Z→A, off). Tooltips on every numeric
header explain the rule behind the number. Dates display **MM-DD-YYYY**.

Above the table: **Only with pending** and **Show withdrawn**. Beside **As of**:
`N employees · N requests to record`.

**Click any employee** → their leave opens in place as a Monday.com-style
subitem table, sorted by date descending:

| Type | Dates | Days | Status | Source | In payroll | Comments | Actions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

- **Type** — PTO (blue) or Floating holiday (violet).
- **In payroll** — what payroll actually recorded across those days, e.g.
  `PTO x4`, `Permiso Remunerado x1`, or `not in payroll yet`. This is the
  cross-check: no need to open the Payroll section to see whether someone took
  the days they asked for.
- **Actions** — **Record** on anything not yet recorded; **Edit** and
  **Withdraw** on recorded rows.

The **Record dialog** shows *Requested on Monday* above *Recording*, so you see
what the employee asked for beside what you are entering. It rejects a return
date before the leave date. There is no "Recorded by" field; new rows are
stamped `recorded_by = 'app'`.

### Floating holidays are leave records now

Not a manual counter. `pto_approvals.leave_type` is `'pto'` or
`'floating_holiday'`; FH used is **derived** from recorded floating-holiday
rows. The 35 Monday "Floating Holiday" requests appear as pending, to be
recorded like any other leave. Someone inside their first 90 days shows a muted
`0` with the eligibility date in a tooltip.

### Navigation

**People** is gone. **PTO Tracker** is a top-level section. Brand caption reads
"HR Hub".

### Admin → Employees → Monday

Directory sync now only offers to *create* people who are in the **Current
Employees** group. Past employees are never offered.

1. Read `docs/HOW-WE-WORK.md` — the plain-language guide to the three places
   the app lives.
2. Read `docs/CHANGE-LOOP.md` — the export → sync → diff → test → browser →
   commit loop. Never hand-edit `src/`.
3. Read `docs/LESSONS.md` — the traps that have actually bitten us, so they
   don't bite again.
4. Read `src/AGENTS.md` — the technical rules (timezone invariant, no
   hardcoded Monday ids, the six files not to touch).
5. Every prompt sent to UIB is on record under
   `docs/superpowers/prompts/<date>-<topic>/`. The specs and plans are under
   `docs/superpowers/specs/` and `docs/superpowers/plans/`.
