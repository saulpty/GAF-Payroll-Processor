**Subject:** Idea: putting the Quarterly Review form online

Hi Jess,

I've been thinking about the quarterly reviews and how much back-and-forth they take: the template goes out by email, managers fill it in, print to PDF, email it to you, and the copies end up in folders. I'd like to propose something I can build for you.

**The idea:** the exact same review form you already use, with the same sections, ratings and look, but online. Managers sign in with their normal work login, fill it out, and hit Submit. Everything is saved in one place, and comparing with last quarter is built in.

I've attached two pictures: **Before** (today's template) and **After** (the proposed version). In the After picture, everything that's new is outlined in yellow. Nothing from the current form is removed.

**Before → After**

| | Today | Proposed |
|---|---|---|
| Filling it out | Download the HTML file, fill in, print to PDF | Open a link, sign in with your work email, fill in |
| Unfinished reviews | Lost if the window is closed | **Save Draft**, come back any time |
| Who's the reviewer | Typed by hand | Filled in automatically from the login |
| Employee name | Typed by hand each quarter | Picked from the manager's own list, so every quarter stays linked to the same person |
| Sending it to HR | Manager writes an email with the subject in the right format and attaches the PDF | **Submit** emails the PDF to the manager, CCs you, and puts the subject in your format ("First Last – Q3 Review") automatically |
| Where reviews live | Inboxes and computer folders | One database, searchable, nothing lost |
| Comparing with last quarter | Dig up last quarter's PDF and compare by hand | Last quarter's score shows next to each rating, and the overall score shows "Q3 was 3.40" |
| Goals | Next-quarter goals are written down, then forgotten until someone finds the old file | Last quarter's "Next Quarter KPIs" **fill in automatically** as this quarter's goals; the manager just enters the result and the score |
| Action plans | Not followed up | Last quarter's action plans show up with "Was it done? Yes / Partly / No" |
| Review history | Scattered | Every employee shows their previous reviews (Q2, Q3…), each with its PDF one click away |

**Ideas I could add if useful to you** (not in the pictures):
- An HR page for you showing which managers have submitted and who's still missing before the October 31 deadline. That would help with the "no bonus without a review" rule.
- Fields for the two things you ask for in the email body, "Feedback from employee" and "Disciplinary action taken (if applicable)", so they're always included automatically.

This is just a proposal. If you like it, I'd start with a few managers before rolling it out to everyone. Happy to walk you through it.
