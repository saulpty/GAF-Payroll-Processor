## Decisions Saul made (this session)

- Managers can act on their pages **except PTO** (no recording for now). Closing a disciplinary case stays allowed.
- Unknown SSO email → No access screen.
- Manager emails are the identity. First load came from the Monday directory; secondary managers are added by hand.
- New hires from the directory sync go to the group whose primary manager's email matches.
- Managers land on Attendance (Employee 360 later).
