# Step 0: fifteen minutes with a real phone (Saul)

Nothing changes in the app. This decides one thing (whether the sections can sit at the bottom of
the screen) and confirms four smaller ones. Do it on the **live** address, signed in. iPhone
(Safari) first; an Android phone (Chrome) too if any manager uses one.

Send back a screenshot for each numbered check, or just "yes" / "no".

| # | Do this | Tell me | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open **Contracts**. Scroll to the very last employee. | Is the last row fully visible above the browser's own bar at the bottom? Screenshot. | If the bottom is cut off, a section bar placed there would be cut off too. Then the sections stay at the top instead (prompt 02 is skipped). |
| 2 | Turn the phone sideways on the same page and scroll to the bottom again. | Same question. | Same reason, sideways. |
| 3 | Look at the top and sides of the screen. | Is there any UI Bakery bar, logo or menu around the app, or only the app itself? Screenshot of the top. | Tells me how much room the app really has. |
| 4 | Scroll to the top of the Contracts list, then pull down with your finger. | Does the whole page reload? | If yes, the fix in prompt 05 is needed (it is already planned). |
| 5 | Open **PTO Tracker** and tap the "Search employee" box. | Does the screen zoom in when the keyboard opens? | Expected: yes. Prompt 05 stops it. |
| 6 | Open **Attendance → Today**, tap any employee so the side panel opens, then press the phone's **Back** (Android: back button; iPhone: swipe from the left edge). | Where do you end up: same page with the panel closed, a different page, or out of the app? | Decides how the full-screen panels must behave with Back. |
| 7 | Leave the app in the background for five minutes, then come back to it. | Does it reload? Does it come back to the page you were on? | Known behaviour of the host; I only need to write it down. |

Also useful: one screenshot each of Attendance → Today, PTO, Contracts and Disciplinary as they
look right now on your phone. They become the "before" pictures.
