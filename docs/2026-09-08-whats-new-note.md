# What's new in the HR Hub — note you can send

Plain language, no jargon. Trim or reword freely; the numbers are the part worth
keeping accurate.

---

**Subject: Attendance updates in the HR Hub**

A few changes went live in the HR Hub today. The one to know about first:

**On-time percentages have moved slightly, and that's deliberate.**

Until now, if someone was scheduled to work, never clocked in, and filed nothing
to explain it, that day was grouped with holidays and PTO and left out of their
attendance score entirely. So a day nobody could account for counted as neither
good nor bad.

Those days now count. Everyone's on-time percentage is recalculated on the same
basis, so most people move by a fraction of a point. A few move more. Nothing
about anyone's actual attendance changed — only what the number was measuring.

**"Excused" is now "Time off",** and it breaks down into what it actually
contains: PTO, holidays, birthday and compensatory days, and approved absences.
Time off has never affected anyone's score and still doesn't.

**Every number now explains itself.** Hover the ⓘ next to any figure and it tells
you what counts toward it. That includes the Good / Fair / At Risk badge, which
is based on on-time rate alone — it doesn't account for absences or missing
forms.

**There's a new "Reporting" column** next to that badge. It shows, out of the
days that needed an explanation, how many had a form filed — for example 6/7. It
is deliberately separate from At Risk, because arriving late and failing to
report it are two different things. Someone can be frequently late and always
file; someone else can be rarely late and never file. Both are now visible.

If you were never late and never absent, that column shows a dash. Nothing to
report is not a failure.

**A new Reports tab** under Attendance shows, day by day, who was late or absent,
the form behind each day, and whether it was submitted before the shift started —
which is what the policy asks for. You can view it as a weekly grid per person or
as a full list.

---

## Two things it has already surfaced

Worth knowing, because people may ask:

**About half of late arrivals have no form on file.** Of 97 late days since
15 August, 47 had no Attendance form. That's not a new problem — it was simply
never visible in one place before.

**Some forms were filed from the wrong email address** and, because of how the
system matched them, were not being seen at all. That's now fixed, and those days
flag on the Reports tab. If your company email on the form doesn't match the one
in the directory, the form may not reach your record — worth checking if you've
filed something that doesn't appear.

---

## If a number looks wrong

Say so. The Reports tab shows the punches, the form and the timing behind every
day, so any figure can be traced back to what it came from.
